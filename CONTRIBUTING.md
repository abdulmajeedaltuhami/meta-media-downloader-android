# Contributing

Contributions are welcome when they remain compatible with the project's
noncommercial license and copyright notice.

Before opening an issue, include Android version, device ABI, app version,
steps to reproduce, and logs with personal URLs and cookies removed.

Do not submit private credentials, exported cookies, copyrighted media, or
links to content you do not have permission to share. Pull requests should
preserve Arabic RTL support, direct Download-folder saving, duplicate
protection, and the noncommercial license.
